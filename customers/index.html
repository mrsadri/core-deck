What we found

Six problems, one missing piece.

Orders, and no one to attach them to
01Work revolves around the order list, not the customer.
02Finding one customer takes too long.
03A customer's information is out of reach.
04After an order, returning or new is unclear.
05Installments and purchase history are hard to read.
06Notes still live in a paper diary.
Problem 01  /  F1
TodayWork revolves around the order list, not the customer.

Every order belongs to a customer.

Each order is linked to one customer record, so the panel has someone to organise around.

Loose orders
One record
Problem 02  /  F2
TodayFinding a specific customer takes too long.

Find anyone in one search.

The customer list searches by name, national ID, phone number and licence plate.

One stone, pulled clear
Problem 03  /  F3
TodayPolicies, documents, installments and contact details are not easily accessible.

One page holds the whole customer.

A comprehensive profile, with everything the partner knows about that person in one place.

Scattered bricks, gathered into one span
Problem 03  /  F5
TodayA customer's policies and documents are spread across separate orders.

Every policy in one place.

All of a customer's policies on the profile, each with its status and a file to download.

The policies, shelved together
Problem 04  /  F4
TodayAfter an order is placed, it is unclear whether the customer is returning or new.

Returning or new, answered at once.

The panel tells the partner which one it is the moment the order is registered.

Returning
New
Problem 05  /  F6
TodayA customer's installment and purchase history is not easy to see.

The money, at a glance.

A view of the customer's financial status, so what is settled and what is outstanding is readable in a moment.

Laid, and still to come
Problem 06  /  F7
Beyond phase 1
TodayPartners log Notes per customer in a physical paper diary.

The paper diary moves in.

Notes logged in the customer profile, next to everything else about that person.

The stones still outside the record
F8
Beyond phase 1
TodayA customer only exists in the panel once an order has been registered.

Add a customer before the first order.

Partners add prospective customers to the list by hand, and work them from there.

One stone set before the span exists
